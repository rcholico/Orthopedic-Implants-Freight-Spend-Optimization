# 🩺 Orthopedic Implants Freight Spend Optimization & Service Level Mode-Shifting (Central Texas Hub)

**Data-Driven Service Level Downgrade Modeling & Ground Mode Shift for $1.36M+ Annual Savings**

An executive freight spend analytics and service-level optimization project conducted for a Medical Tech leader (originating in Central Texas facilities), analyzing Parcel Carrier Air vs. Ground trade-offs and overnight delivery commitment downgrades to optimize parcel spend.

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Medical device freight often defaults to premium Express Air services due to perceived urgency. This project analyzed historical parcel data from the company's facilities in Central Texas to quantify cost differentials between Express Air and Ground, modeling the financial return of adding 1 delivery day or adjusting morning delivery commitment times.

---

## 📊 Strategic Optimization Levers & Financial Impact

### Lever 1: Air-to-Ground Mode Shift (1–3 Day Transit Contours)
Shifted eligible air express shipments to Parcel Ground where ground transit time matched or added only 1 day of lead time:
* **3-Day Ground Transit Contours (24 States):** Adding 1 day of transit time across 24 destination states yielded a **60% rate reduction** (reducing quarterly spend from $207.4K down to $78.1K, saving **$129.3K/quarter or $517.3K/year**).
* **High-Volume State Savings in CA, FL & NY:** Generated an additional **$55K/quarter ($220K/year)** in rate savings.
* **1–2 Day Transit Contours:** Generated an additional **$128K/quarter ($512K/year)** in rate savings.

### Lever 2: Overnight Service Level Downgrade (First Overnight 8 AM $\rightarrow$ Priority 11 AM)
Re-evaluated the necessity of the Parcel Carrier's First Overnight service vs their Priority Overnight:
* **100% Conversion to Priority Overnight:** Generates **$369,000 / year in direct savings**.
* **90% Conversion to Priority Overnight:** Generates **$332,000 / year in direct savings**.
